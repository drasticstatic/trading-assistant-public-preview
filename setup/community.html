TMade × TheGexLab

TMade partnered with Yush to build TheGexLab — real-time dealer positioning (put/call walls, zero gamma, and the regime read that says whether the market's pinned or primed to run) laid directly onto the chart. Their Asia-session read is nicknamed "night-vision" — which lands close to home, since our own ETH-derived ZTH levels in the auto-levels indicator render green vs. cyan for exactly that reason. Years of building this system, and it's still finding new confluence to lean on.

Partner access: thegexlab.com/tmade ↗ — code TMADE for 20% off. This is a gated partner link, not the public marketing site.
