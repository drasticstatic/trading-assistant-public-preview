Prop Firm Rules & Pre-Flight Checklist

A quick visual comparison across every prop firm Christopher trades or tracks, plus a daily pre-flight checklist before the market opens. Figures shown are the 50K-tier headline numbers — always verify current rules against the full reference docs linked at the bottom, since prop firm rules change frequently.

Daily Pre-Flight Checklist

Run through before the open — especially on multi-account days

  • Confirm which account(s) are active today and their exact close deadlineApex ~16:59 ET · TPT 16:55 ET auto-close · TopOneFutures 16:10 ET · Lucid 16:45 ET
  • SL placed and never moved — Pattern 7 discipline checkSee pattern_tracker.md for the full pattern history
  • No opposing/hedged positions across accountsApex, TPT, and TopOneFutures all treat cross-account hedging as an instant, all-accounts-involved violation
  • Daily Loss Limit checked, if the account has oneApex EOD/PA, Tradeify, some Lucid configs — TPT and Apex Intraday have none
  • Consistency rule tracked if trading toward a payoutTPT/Lucid eval 50% · Tradeify 35% · TopOneFutures funded 40%
  • Check today's calendar for FOMC / CPI / NFP before the openTPT blackout ±1 min · TopOneFutures blackout only if a position is modified ±2 min · Apex/Lucid Flex/Pro/Direct allow it · LucidDaily hard-forbids red-folder news
  • Cancel all resting/projected orders before stepping awayPattern 9 — unintentional fills from stale limit orders
  • Personal hard-exit time set before the firm's deadline becomes the exit mechanismPattern 8 — never let the prop firm's forced close be the trade management plan
FOMC / CPI / NFP — News Blackout Rules by Firm

Rules diverge sharply by firm — some fully permit news trading, others carry a hard 1-2 minute blackout window. Know which account is live before a red-folder release

TakeProfitTrader PRO / PRO+

Forbidden 1 minute before, during, and 1 minute after FOMC / CPI / NFP

TopOneFutures

Forbidden only if a position is modified 2 minutes before/during/after — holding through untouched is fine

Apex Trader Funding

  • News-event trading is allowed as part of a normal strategy
  • Prohibited: pure gambling, "chasing the market," or opposing bracket orders placed to luck into a news breakout
  • No hedging — simultaneous long+short on the same/correlated instrument is a standing hard rule, separate from news

Lucid Trading

  • LucidFlex / Pro / Direct: news trading fully allowed — enter/exit freely around major releases, no penalty
  • LucidDaily: trading red-folder (high-impact) news is a hard breach, not allowed on this variant
  • Market risk (slippage/unexpected execution) still applies during news on both platforms regardless of the rule
Daily Close Deadlines — Know Which Account You're In

Every firm sets an automatic cutoff to protect both the firm and the trader — a safety net, not a trap. Enforcement style differs though: some auto-flatten cleanly, others carry the possibility of account failure if the close coincides with a slippage/gap event. Confirm which bucket today's account is in before assuming the same habit transfers across accounts

Copy-Trading

The short version across every firm on file: copying your own trade decision from one of your own accounts to another is generally fine; mirroring someone else's signals/calls is not

TopOneFutures explicitly allows copy trading between your own accounts across firms, as long as the trade decision originates on the TOF account itself — mirroring someone else's signals (Discord/Telegram calls, group trading) is prohibited. See TOF's Copy Trading Policy ↗ and the fuller prohibited-practices list in topone-rules.md ↗

Reset Economics

Cost-of-staying-in numbers, for the accounts Christopher actually runs

TakeProfitTrader: a reset costs $649, capped at 3 resets total. Pricier than starting a fresh instant-funded account elsewhere once you're past reset #1-2 — but Christopher values staying with TPT despite the account-count ceiling, so a reset is still the default move over starting over cold.

Apex Legacy renewal nuance: renewing a Legacy account rolls existing profits into the next 30-day cycle rather than zeroing them out. New Apex products (EOD/Intraday) don't carry this — missing the 30-day profit target on those simply fails the account, no renewal-with-profits-intact option
