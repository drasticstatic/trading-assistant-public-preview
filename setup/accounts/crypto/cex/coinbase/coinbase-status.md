# Coinbase Advanced — Status

> Full regulatory analysis: [us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md](../us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md)

**Account held:** Yes — active, testing, TradingView-connected (spot + derivatives).
**Regulatory status:** Fully CFTC-compliant. Coinbase is a registered Futures Commission Merchant (FCM); "US Perpetual-Style Futures" run natively inside the domestic Coinbase Advanced dashboard.
**What's tradeable:** Spot, plus perpetual-style futures (long-dated, funding-rate-anchored contracts functioning like true perps without a monthly roll).
**Key facts:** Leverage capped at 10x intraday for digital assets (vs. up to 50x on Coinbase's offshore entity for non-US users). USDC supported directly as collateral. Fees as low as 0.02%/contract. One of the two genuinely compliant, retail-friendly US perp paths alongside Kraken Pro.
