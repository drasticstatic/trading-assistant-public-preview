# Crypto.com — Status

> Full regulatory analysis: [us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md](../us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md)

**Account held:** Yes — active, testing, TradingView-connected (spot only).
**Regulatory status:** The global Crypto.com Exchange's crypto perps are strictly geoblocked for US residents. The US entity, Crypto.com | Derivatives North America (CDNA), is built on the Nadex exchange acquisition.
**What's tradeable:** Spot on the main exchange. CDNA offers traditional derivatives (binary options, standard quarterly crypto futures) — no continuous crypto perpetuals for US retail.
**Key facts:** Crypto.com's US strategy leans toward SEC/CFTC-registered single-stock futures and stock-based perpetuals rather than launching crypto perps domestically — its US layout fits prediction markets/binary options/traditional stock contracts better than perp trading.
