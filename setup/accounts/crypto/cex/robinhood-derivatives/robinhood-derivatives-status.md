# Robinhood Derivatives (crypto angle) — Status

> Full regulatory analysis: [us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md](../us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md). Note: this covers Robinhood's crypto-futures/prediction-market angle specifically — the existing equities/options Robinhood account is tracked separately (`project_exchange_roster` memory).

**Account held:** Robinhood account exists (equities/options); the crypto-derivatives side specifically not actively used yet.
**Regulatory status:** Robinhood Derivatives, LLC is a registered CFTC FCM. Current offering: standard (non-perpetual) CME Bitcoin/Ethereum futures, plus crypto prediction markets cleared via partners including Kalshi — not true perpetuals yet.
**What's tradeable:** Traditional expiring crypto futures, event/prediction contracts. No perps.
**Key facts:** Robinhood has stated intent to launch fully regulated domestic perps and took an equity stake in OG.com toward that CFTC approval. Separately, the standalone Robinhood Wallet's perps tab (via the Lighter DEX) is blocked for US residents — Lighter has no CFTC clearance. Watch for a future perp launch rather than treating this as active today.
