# BTCC — Status

> Full regulatory analysis: [us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md](../us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md)

**Account held:** Yes — active, main perp venue, TradingView-connected for execution.
**Regulatory status:** Offshore, no CFTC registration — legally gray for US derivatives. Already KYC-verified as a US resident, which removes the "unverified account frozen on withdrawal" failure mode most US traders hit.
**What's tradeable:** Perpetual futures only (no spot).
**Key facts:** Withdraw via blockchain (USDT/BTC/ETH) to a personal wallet or a US-regulated exchange, never fiat — match networks exactly (e.g. TRC-20 both ends), test-withdraw a small amount first. Treat continued access as provisional; an offshore platform can geoblock existing US accounts overnight under regulatory pressure.
