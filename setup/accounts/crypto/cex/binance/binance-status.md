# Binance (US + Global) — Status

> Full regulatory analysis: [us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md](../us-crypto-derivatives-regulatory-landscape.md)

**Account held:** Yes — Binance.US only, spot.
**Regulatory status:** Binance.US is a fully separate entity from global Binance, operating under a strict crypto-only spot framework with zero CFTC derivatives clearance — **no perps exist on Binance.US at all**, not a restriction to work around, just not offered. Global Binance has one of the largest perp markets in the world but aggressively geoblocks and KYC-screens the US; VPN access triggers automated compliance flags and can lock capital behind a non-US verification wall.
**What's tradeable:** Spot only, on the US entity.
**Key facts:** Don't conflate the two brands — holding a Binance.US account provides zero derivatives access, and global Binance is not a viable path regardless.
