Sources: the waitlist page ([tradeifyperps.co](https://tradeifyperps.co/), beta-key sign-up only, Cloudflare-gated) and a third-party launch write-up (PropFirmCircle, Oct 23, 2026). **Nothing below is from a Tradeify rulebook yet**; treat the numbers as the launch coverage's claims (⚠️) until the beta opens and the terms can be read.

Maker 0.02% / taker 0.05%, hourly funding.
- **Why it matters here:** a second perps prop venue on the same Hyperliquid feeds the terminal already reads (no venue offset to manage, unlike MFP's Binance-priced markets), static drawdown like MFP Elite, and 24/7. It fits the "Fortuna on prop perps" lane next to MFP once rules are public.
