## 📈 Week Arc — The Full Picture

Monday and Tuesday's trades (MES +$12.50, two MNQ fills totaling +$494.00) are reconstructed directly from Tradovate order-fill exports — no session journal, Apple Notes, or Littlebird context exists for that window yet, so the behavioral texture of those two days stays pending. The numbers are real; the story around them isn't fabricated ahead of the data.

Thursday broke that pattern — not with a trade, but with the day the manual multi-device execution method (MacBook, iPhone, and iMac running simultaneously, alternating live between TPT and TopOneFutures by hand) finally got retired. **TradeCopia went live**: six accounts connected, two copy groups built, and every risk-relevant number chosen deliberately rather than defaulted — TopOneFutures' actual copy-trading policy ruled out the original account pairing outright (confirmed by TOF's own assistant, not assumed), Apex's "submit for approval" requirement turned out not to apply once actually asked, and a $600 daily-loss instinct got revised down to a flat $555 across all six accounts the moment the question turned from "does this feel safe" to "would this actually have saved the account." Group A's leader and follower swapped mid-session too — not for compliance reasons, but because a 1-micro-contract sizing habit was quietly making half the group's positions round down to zero, and the fix cost something real (TPT's automatic news-rule gate) that's now being covered by hand instead of by structure.

This week's real story isn't the +$506.50 on the board — it's that the account ledger's own framing, *"what once looked like a graveyard is a resurrection project,"* picked up its next real piece of infrastructure. The recovery arc started Feb 13, 2026, with a blowup and no rule held. Thursday added the first automated backstop for a specific failure mode that discipline alone has never fully covered: being physically unable to close a trade in time. Full detail: [Sep 3 daily review](https://github.com/drasticstatic/trading-assistant-public-preview/blob/main/fortuna-exports/overview-summaries/2026/09-Sep/export_20260903_daily-review.md).

Also worth marking, for context rather than depth: **Mystarch**, an app-level Chief-of-Staff persona, joined the broader agent lineup this week alongside Fortuna, Auggie, and Alfred — not built inside this repo, just noted here the way this project tries to document the "why" behind the work, not only the "what."
